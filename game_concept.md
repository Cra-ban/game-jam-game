# Sam altman stole my ram (working title)

## Style
- **Artstyle:** weird indie unhinged (hylics esque)
- **Game Type:** Point and click (popochiu)

**sam altman** stole ur ram, and ur computer doesnt work anymore now so u have t get it back from the datacenter and also destroy it in the process
